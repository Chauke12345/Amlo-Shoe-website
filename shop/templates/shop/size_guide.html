How To Measure Your Foot

  1. Place a sheet of paper on a flat floor against a wall.
  2. Stand on the paper with your heel gently touching the wall.
  3. Mark the point where your longest toe ends.
  4. Measure from the edge of the paper to your mark in centimetres.
  5. Compare your measurement with the AMLO size guide above.
